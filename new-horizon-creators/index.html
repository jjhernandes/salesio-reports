Section 05Honest read

A Straight Read on Show Rate

The part that went the wrong way, and what we're doing about it.

05

Every call is worth far more now, but fewer calls are happening. Show rate slipped from 83.3% to 76.7% since Sep 18, and bookings fell from about 14.6 to 10.4 a day (452 in the 31 days before, 397 in the 38 days since). Part of the "before" number was a one-off: a giveaway brought in 276 bookings in August alone. But prospects are also cancelling more (83 cancellations by prospects before, 98 since). Calls held went from 179 to 148. Better closing more than made up for it this time. It won't keep making up for it forever. There's also a tracking gap: in the last 14 days, 139 calls were on the calendar but only 61 have a logged result, so recent show and close rates may still move as results get filled in. Getting show rate back to 83% is lever 1, and it's worth about $17k a month on its own.

Data sources & notes

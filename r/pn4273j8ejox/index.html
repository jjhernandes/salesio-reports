The short version

What we covered

Iwin asked about people-pleasing and why people do it, and the call stayed on that versus real confidence. JJ's take is that people-pleasing can win people over, but only temporarily, and the respect that lasts comes from being authentic, not from chasing validation. The amount of value you give other people's opinions is what puts power over you. Iwin talked about over-promising, setting the bar high, and trying to be the person who can do it all, which is part of what pulls him into people-pleasing. He also named the gap he feels between how he is in a normal conversation and how he shows up on prospect calls, where it starts to feel like a performance and wears him down after a couple of calls. JJ's direction was to stop performing, give himself permission to be himself, and look at what he is doing that he does not need to keep doing. The practical step: after each call, write out how it went and why it did or did not go his way, including the wins and the losses, so they can see what's underneath, like intention or connection. JJ said Iwin is on the right track, to keep doing what he is doing right now, and not to overthink it. They can review those notes together.

The short version

What we covered

Brody Robb joined JJ for an interview for the Content Academy setting team. After a quick catch-up, Brody asked how the team is run and why it is not hiring more setters. JJ explained that the team is about five months into sales management, went from roughly 160-170k to 400k in 27 days, and is at 84k for the week on a Wednesday. Two setters are on the team, and JJ prefers a lean group where setters are not drowning in opportunity and everyone makes good money. Brody then shared his background: about 10 months in high ticket sales, a little under 400k lifetime cash collected, a show rate of at least 85%, and some closing experience. He said he would commit fully to the team if brought on. JJ said no skills test was needed, that he would give Justin the green light, and that Brody could start this week or Monday.

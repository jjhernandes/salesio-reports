The short version

What we covered

Mikaela raised a show-rate concern: prospects get triaged by phone after the webinar, then texted and dialed again on the day of the sales call. One prospect said she had confirmed days earlier, showed up, and found no one on the call, and was not impressed. Kirsty floated replacing day-of dialing with a quick voice note asking for a thumbs up. JJ pulled up the lead in GHL, said the automations need fixing more than anything, and asked Mikaela to send the lead details. Mikaela said another call right before the sales call can feel like too many touchpoints and may push prospects away. JJ agreed: after a triage, a second confirmation call is not necessary, and a short bump text is enough if you want one. The group also touched briefly on mindset and book recommendations at the start.

Bottom line

What matters

The decision is to cut back on touchpoints. A prospect who has already been triaged and confirmed does not need to be dialed again right before the call. A soft bump text offering a call is the most that is needed, and the data will show whether pushback becomes a pattern.

The open question is the automations. Mikaela could not tell whether the day-of confirmation messages are going out, and JJ said that is the piece to check. Leave same-day confirmation to the automations until that is verified.

What to fix

Stop stacking touchpoints

Say it like this“Hey, we spoke a day ago. Just want to make sure you’re good for your call today. Any questions for me? Let me know if you’d like me to give you a call.”

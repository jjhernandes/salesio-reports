What we covered

Stress Management & Mindset

  • Core Problem: Stress and self-punishment over setbacks like lost deals, fluctuating income, or comparison bias. ▶ 3:04
  • JJ's $2M Deal Loss: ▶ 4:04
  • A signed $2M/year deal with an influencer was canceled last Friday.
  • Initial Reaction: A physical shock and urge to be upset.
  • Mindset Shift: Consciously chose gratitude over anger, focusing on the opportunity and lessons learned.
  • Outcome: The deal returned today, delayed by two weeks.
  • Insight: An upset reaction would have ruined the week and potentially closed the door on the deal's return.
  • Maintaining Momentum: ▶ 26:14
  • Realistic Goals: Base targets on data to create belief and internal urgency.
  • Strategic Comparison: Be selective about who you compare yourself to.
  • Self-Awareness: Identify your personal operating style (e.g., structured vs. unstructured).

Sales Systems & AI

  • Problem: Many founders avoid systems, prioritizing revenue over structure. JJ, a self-identified "number two" (unstructured), now advocates for systems. ▶ 39:07
  • Solution: A Simple Notion OS ▶ 42:18
  • A central Notion workspace provides a single source of truth for all company operations.
  • Key Components:
  • AI-Powered Automation: ▶ 47:47
  • Fathom → Notion: Fathom transcripts are fed to Claude, which generates to-do lists in Notion. This creates external urgency for unstructured individuals.
  • Claude for SOPs: Claude drafts SOPs from prompts, requiring only a quick review. This saves significant time compared to manual creation.
  • Claude for Zapier: Claude can build complex Zapier automations, eliminating the frustration of manual setup.

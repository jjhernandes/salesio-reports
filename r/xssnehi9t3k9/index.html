The short version

What we covered

Mino opened by asking how he could show up more for the team, which led to the idea of a larger package for prospects who object to done-for-you. Malachi's version was a weekly group call with Mino, one with Justin, and a biweekly 1-on-1 for 90 days. JJ pushed on what else could move the needle, and Mino pointed at show rate, currently 45% to 50%. A team member shared that one closer was getting prospects to send a short video or voice note instead of a long back-and-forth, and the group built on it with an urgency-based follow-up and a group chat that introduces the closer. They also debated adding a fifth closer, agreed to start interviewing, and settled on a group interview Thursday with a trial starting Monday. Mino said he is open to daily webinars, JJ suggested calling existing members as a check-in that leads to an upsell, and they landed on BAM of 40k per closer and 62.5k as the reach. Pricing for the 1-on-1 landed at $25k, tested through Mino's story first.

Bottom line

What matters

The plan is to raise urgency on both ends at once: content urgency on the front (a limited 1-on-1 offer announced on Mino's story, only five spots) and a hard push on the back from the team, who go back through their follow-ups to find the prospects who asked for more access. The test offer is $25k for 90 days: a biweekly 1-on-1 with Mino, one weekly group call with Mino and one with Justin. It needs at least one close to count as proof of concept.

Alongside the offer, the team is adding a fifth closer with a group interview Thursday and a trial starting next Monday, running more webinars (Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday), and lifting the show rate from about 50% toward 60% with video or voice-note context and a three-way group chat intro to the closer.

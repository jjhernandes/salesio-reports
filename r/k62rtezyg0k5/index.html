The short version

What we covered

Jia finished week one, which was three videos done in about an hour, and the state-management lesson resonated. JJ used the call to find her baseline on the map of consciousness. Jia placed herself around neutrality and willingness, with reason as the highest level she feels she reaches and desire pulling her down when she compares where she is with where she wants to be. JJ explained that desire and anger usually come from comparison, and gave a simple move back up the scale: ask why someone did something instead of judging them. He also made the point that sales people are more used to chaos than to peace, so the first job is awareness. The assignment for the week is to track where time, attention and energy go and what brings her up or down. JJ shared extra lesson material privately, added her to the student community, and set up a recurring weekly call. Recaps and next steps will come through Circle.

Bottom line

What matters

JJ's core message: do not chase a perfect answer, just get honest about where you operate from day to day, because every later lesson builds on that.

He framed it as awareness first. Where the time goes, where the attention goes, and whether the basic things you already know you need to do feel blocked. If energy is low, it is usually because time and attention are scattered.

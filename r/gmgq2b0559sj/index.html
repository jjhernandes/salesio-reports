The short version

What we covered

The prospect runs an AI agency selling automation to small and mid-sized businesses. Delivery is no longer a problem, but his sales calls convert at an estimated 10 to 15 percent and he cannot tell whether the issue is his skills or his offer. JJ explained that sophisticated B2B buyers need to see an expert, and that a pitch built around a clear demo of one outcome works better than a slide deck of guarantees. He also raised the possibility that the prospect is too nice with direct buyers. The prospect walked through his call process and pitch on screen. JJ described the 12 week one-on-one program, with Evan available as a B2B coach, and sent a link to the free community. The prospect asked for 20 to 24 hours to research and is comparing one other coach. They planned to speak again the next day.

Bottom line

What matters

The core gap is perception. As JJ put it, buyers are "low competence, meaning they don't really understand what it is that you're selling, and they need you to be an expert." When they do not see that expert, they stall.

The fix is to show the one outcome the buyer wants and prove it with a short demo. The decision now sits with the prospect after his research window.

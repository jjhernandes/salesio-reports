What we covered

Jia Le Zhen has been in sales since May, mostly as a setter and dialer, and now wants to move into closing. The last few weeks were spent quoting permanent lights over the phone, which is not the direction Jia wants. JJ asked what kind of opportunity Jia is after, and Jia said the niche matters less than strong fulfillment and a leader worth following. JJ explained the 12 week curriculum, the week three approval before closing placements, the job board, DealFuel and the internal teams, then showed the platform on screen. After the walkthrough Jia chose to join and JJ set the first 1-on-1 for Saturday at 1pm JJ's time (2pm for Jia), with the first week of curriculum due before then.

Bottom line

What matters

Jia does not need a niche to get started. What matters is picking an opportunity with a founder worth following, and that clarity shapes where Jia gets placed. Closing placements are earned: finish the curriculum, pass the approval at week three, then JJ vouches for Jia.

Jia was honest that role plays against other students have not been productive and that a clear process is the missing piece. JJ wants to see how Jia runs a call in the first session, even if it is only through the script, because he thinks Jia is further along than Jia believes.

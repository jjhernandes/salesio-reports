The short version

What we covered

JJ introduced Jonathan Gabb, a mindset coach who has worked with sales teams, then handed him the floor. Jonathan asked the closers what internal blocks they see beyond scripts and tactics, and framed them as beliefs, emotions and habits. The closers named guilt when pitching the 12k, 8k and 5k offers, early judgment of prospects, projecting their own path onto skeptical prospects, avoiding uncomfortable conversations, and fear of authority or of being judged. Faris asked how to pitch with conviction after several price changes. JJ said the team is strong and hungry, and that reaching the next revenue level needs a subconscious opening around what they believe is possible. Jonathan closed by explaining his method: identify the story, then override it with custom meditations and visualization repeated consistently. He will put together a game plan and speak with JJ after the call.

Bottom line

What matters

The team already has the tactics and the frameworks. The question on this call was what sits underneath them: the beliefs and emotions that show up on a sales call without anyone choosing them. Jonathan's point was that a closer can know every script and still pull back at the close if the pattern underneath is not addressed.

No commitment was made on the call. The next step is Jonathan's game plan and a conversation with JJ about what working with the team could look like.

The short version

What we covered

The call opened with the Friday check-in. Thursday cash collected was lower, and closers pointed to a lot of no-shows and reschedules. Justin walked through the day's calendar, and the guidance was to pitch regular Academy unless a prospect clearly fits the Frontier offer, with the offer doc open but no screen share. Closers asked to see Instagram DM context, and Justin said he would add them to SetDM. The team then ran wins and shoutouts, with JJ naming Allan Luna the all-time top closer. In the second half, Allan and Chris from Social Era joined to explain the new offer: a $1,500 break-even trial month, then an upsell to a three-month package 30 days after the ads go live. Iwin raised the objection that prospects fixate on the $3,750 after-trial price, and JJ coached the framing.

Bottom line

What matters

The new offer works only if the trial is framed as standard practice. Everyone gets a trial first, the price is $1,500, and the after-trial options come up once they say yes or at onboarding. That keeps the call short and removes the sticker shock.

The incentive is built in. Closers sell the trial up front, then check in during the first 30 days and come back for a three-month renewal, which Chris showed working on a recent client call.

What to fix

Prospects only hear the $3,750

Iwin said that when he says $1,500 then $3,750 after results, prospects drop everything before the second number. JJ: present the trial as standard practice and use their objection as the reason for it.

Say it like this"So with everybody we do work with, you run a trial first. So before you commit to anything with us, the starting price is $1,500 for everything."
▶ 46:59

Do not lead with the after-trial price

Close on the $1,500 first. If they ask what happens after, say it is a fair question, confirm the trial makes sense, and walk them through the options during onboarding.

Say it like this"Does $1,500 work for you? Does it make sense?"
▶ 50:29

Hold the line on one trial month

One prospect asked for $1,500 for three months to test results, and another said results would not come in a month. The offer stays one trial month, with billing starting four weeks after the ads go live.

▶ 56:19

Calendar colors do not show the offer

Malachi asked for color coding so closers know which offer they are hopping on for. Justin said that if someone has no offer fit, pitch regular Academy.

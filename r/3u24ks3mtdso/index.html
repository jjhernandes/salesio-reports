The short version

What we covered

The Content Academy sales team watched back a call Malachi had not closed. Malachi opened by saying he challenged the lead too aggressively near the end, and that after talking it through with Allan he now pre-frames challenges and closed his next two calls. The group then replayed the call from the start. The early rapport was good, but the lead was mostly sharing her background, and the call jumped from chit-chat into a pitch. When she said "that's it?", Malachi challenged her on the webinar instead of giving her the information. JJ pushed on where that reaction comes from and tied it to frame control. Faris added that the lead needed broader help than content and that discovery let her ramble. The session ended with JJ stepping away to reset his computer and handing host to Malachi.

Bottom line

What matters

The call was lost in the gap between rapport and pitch. There was no real discovery, so the pitch had nothing to attach to, and the "that's it?" reply set off a challenge that ended the sale.

The fix is two habits: keep the pitch relaxed and let it follow what the lead actually said she needs, and when something throws you off, stay in frame and answer rather than push back. As JJ put it, frame control is not being affected by what is happening around you.

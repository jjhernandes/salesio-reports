The short version

What we covered

Ethan finished week one and the call centered on the map of consciousness. JJ asked what lifts Ethan up and what drags him down, and the two agreed Ethan will track those patterns over the next week. JJ explained that time and attention decide energy, meaning your willingness to keep pushing, and that behaviors can be shifted ahead of the situations that wear you down. Ethan asked how ambition fits with peace, since the higher he went on the scale the less drive he felt. JJ's answer was that you can be pulled instead of pushed: peace can sit under a mission you actually want, and the work is to find the one thing you truly want, which can change over time. JJ gave Ethan a personal link to his custom material and the student portal, mentioned the next Mastermind may be in New York before year end, and they agreed to set up a recurring Friday call.

Bottom line

What matters

JJ's core message: money and anger can push you, but a clear mission pulls you, and pulling lasts longer.

The practical step is to get specific about what brings you up and what brings you down, then shift behavior before the trigger shows up, the same way you pre-frame an objection on a sales call.

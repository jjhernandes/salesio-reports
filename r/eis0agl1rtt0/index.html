The short version

What we covered

The team watched parts of two recorded calls. On the first, a prospect had to bring in a second decision maker who was distracted and not in a buying state. JJ said that call was won on conviction and an expert frame, and that most closers should go down the pain point instead of copying that style. His coaching was to slow down, get the second person away from distractions, and earn the right to speak to them before pitching. On the second call, with an agency owner who said he was only considering content, JJ pointed out the skepticism hiding behind that word and said to probe it, stay in expert frame and not be bro-ish. The team also decided to try a girl's name on the setter outreach for tomorrow's webinar, since show rate has been low. For the closer interview webinar, the email goes out at 5. JJ wants Allan and Omar on it, and if they are not free, Harrison and Malachi.

Bottom line

What matters

Both calls had the same lesson. Do not treat a yes, a maybe or the word considering at face value. Get the real decision maker in a buying state, then ask what would convince them.

Tomorrow's webinar will test a different caller name on the outreach to see if it lifts show rate. The closer interview webinar goes out at 5.

What we covered

Event Performance & Lead Quality

  • A test event yielded zero sales, prompting this review to diagnose issues before a larger event next month. ▶ 1:07
  • Lead quality was the primary challenge: ▶ 2:17
  • Unqualified: Leads with negative bank/credit card balances.
  • Unserious: One lead, "Dolphin Girl Dove," had an unrealistic business idea (translating animal language).
  • Qualified but Mismatched: A lead with a successful physical product business ($250k/yr) was outside the ideal client profile (ICP) of online businesses.

The Standard Pitch vs. Lead Needs

  • The standard 4-pillar pitch (for a $5k, 10-week coaching program) is designed to build an online business from scratch: ▶ 16:37
  • Define ICP
  • Content Strategy (frameworks, hooks)
  • Offer Rebuilding
  • Lead Magnet & Funnel Optimization
  • This pitch is ineffective for established businesses like the cleaning product lead, who already have a successful product, offer, and systems. ▶ 18:29
  • Pitching the wrong pillars triggered the lead's ego and resistance, preventing a close. ▶ 19:28

Recommended Solution: Targeted Discovery

  • JJ recommended a more tactical approach: use deep discovery to identify the single most vital pillar for the lead and focus the pitch there. ▶ 18:04
  • This strategy avoids ego-driven resistance by acknowledging the lead's existing success and targeting their specific pain point. ▶ 19:28
  • Example: The cleaning product lead's pain was poor brand control on Amazon (unauthorized sellers, inconsistent pricing). A pitch focused on content strategy to build direct-to-consumer brand equity would have been more effective than one on offer rebuilding. ▶ 10:37

The short version

What we covered

The call opened with Malachi's video hitting 4,600 views overnight and Justin sharing final numbers for the week. The team beat the weekly cash collected target even though individual goals (Harrison at 50k, Omar at 70k, Allan at a 60% close rate) were missed. JJ asked what was in each person's control. Malachi said he should ask for help faster, and Harrison said his show rate dropped on Sunday and Monday and he could have called and texted more. Justin pushed for everyone to use GrokBot with SendBlue for follow-ups and set homework for Allan and Diego. He also set texting rules after numbers were flagged as spam: no bot wording in messages and no links, videos or photos until the lead replies once. The team shared how they use Fathom transcripts with Mino AI for follow-ups, then set a BAM and reach for the week. Justin added a bonus for hitting your band twice in the second half of the month.

Bottom line

What matters

The point JJ kept returning to is that a target only works if the person setting it believes they can hit it. A goal that is too far past ability breaks performance, so the team set their own numbers this week rather than being handed one. BAM is the bare minimum you put your name on and reach is the stretch.

Follow-ups are moving toward automation. GrokBot with SendBlue gives faster replies than a person, as long as the texting rules are followed so the numbers stay out of spam. JJ will send the team a prompt to cut usage costs.

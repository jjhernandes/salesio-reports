The short version

What we covered

Jia opened by saying they are dropping a setter offer and going all in on applying for closing roles, after working through the job board course and a recruiter training on looms, interviews and follow-ups. JJ explained that failure is normal and walked through the anti-fragility system: set a desired outcome, name the failure you expect, gather resources, move with initiation and speed, hit a breaking point, then iterate. After a few examples it clicked. Jia and JJ set the desired outcome as making 10k as a closer, with the expected failure being only low OTE closing offers or setting offers. JJ gave permission to apply to full cycle roles but not closer-only roles yet, and asked Jia to send a loom before applying anywhere. JJ will personalize the anti-fragility doc, review the loom, and connect Jia with people to role-play with.

Bottom line

What matters

The goal is a closing role, and the plan treats failure as part of the process. A miss, like 30 applications that only turn into setting offers, is a signal to change resources and speed, not proof that Jia is not good enough.

JJ is putting his name behind Jia on full cycle roles and wants the loom first so he can shape it. Closer-only applications wait until the interview prep is further along.

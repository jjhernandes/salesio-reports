What we covered

Ryley hopped on an impromptu 1:1 and asked how to shift his mindset around live transfers. When a call no-shows, the resistance shows up and he does something else instead, then feels guilty after. JJ's coaching is to look at the net ROI, time spent versus money made, and test it over a week or a month, including which hours matter most, so the reason to go get the transfer is clear. Sales is naturally reactive, so you have to create artificial urgency. Visualization helps you feel the goal so it pulls you toward the work. Do that daily. Ryley also said this month has been rough: 40 calls sent, five closes. He is a new pod leader with a team of seven, and he asked how to train them when they are not sure what they need. JJ will send some initial advice on that.

The short version

What we covered

Ed Menlove, who manages closers on one of the biggest offers in the space, opened with the numbers behind his team: about 4.5M a month, with two closers over 100K a month in commissions. He then tied the group's form answers to three themes. First, internal standards: to earn 30K to 50K a month you have to operate like a 30K to 50K closer, which means preparing like an athlete, drilling discovery, objections and the pitch until they are automatic, and focusing fully on the prospect. Second, landing offers: find your unfair advantage, build a Loom that opens with energy and moves into a slide deck with proof, and ladder up from a first offer instead of waiting for a perfect one. Third, auditing your problems: recency bias makes closers chase the last objection they lost, when the real cause is usually upstream in discovery. He also covered how to ramp onto a new offer by watching calls, and took questions in the chat.

Bottom line

What matters

The gap Ed saw in the room is not skill, it is standards and diagnosis. Most people on the call set a 30K to 50K a month target but prepare at a level that does not match it, and many name a problem that is a symptom, not the cause.

The fix is the same in both cases: raise the intensity of preparation, learn the process well enough to run it at double speed in your head, and look at the whole process, including other reps' calls, before deciding what is holding you back.

What to fix

Do not let recency bias pick your problem

Losing the same objection a few times in a row makes it feel like the problem. Most of the time it is upstream, in discovery. A wife objection, for example, usually points to value or motivation not being built earlier in the call.

▶ 46:37

Make the Loom more than a talking head

Start with energy on camera, move into a slide deck with real proof like screenshots of prospect praise and cash collected, and end with a QR code to a 15 minute meeting. Record it a few times.

▶ 26:50

Show the metrics hiring managers care about

Lead with lifetime cash collected and take-home commission rather than close rate.

▶ 27:54

Ask the hiring manager for their top struggle, then role-play it

Finish the interview by asking what reps struggle with most, offer to handle it live, and then ask for the job.

Say it like thisWould you like me to show you how I would handle that now?

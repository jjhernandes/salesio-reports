The short version

What we covered

JJ opened by sharing the Travel with Jaro brand, the culture and the large pool of past and new leads. Faris explained the plan: ads will push to surveys to book calls, dialers get five lead lists, and the team meets every Monday with short technical check-ins through the week. The offer ranges from $3,997 to $20K with the core at $5,997, and the focus is the US and Canada on Eastern time. Dialers work six days a week, claim leads first come, first serve, and should aim for $150 a day using 45 minute dialing segments with the Tally Counter extension in Chrome. Faris will finalize the SOP and get executive approval so dialing can begin as early as tomorrow. JJ then onboarded everyone into Slack and GHL.

Bottom line

What matters

The dialing team is new for NHC, so there are no touch points yet and the early weeks are about building the system. Patience matters while ads ramp and lists fill.

The aim is effective dialing, not time on the calendar. Short focused segments in peak pickup hours, clean tracking and lots of questions to Faris are what get dialers to the daily KPI.

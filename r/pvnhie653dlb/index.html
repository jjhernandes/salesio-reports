The short version

What we covered

JJ opened by saying everyone had sent back their notifications, which made next steps clear. Starting Monday the team will run a short rapid-fire review with Josh, James and Jalen, with content, funnel and affiliate KPIs for each person so output can be tied to revenue. Josh said the software is close and the downloaded version looks good, which means KPIs can go out to Evan, Jalen, Lu and the affiliates. JJ wants a fast push for the rest of the month, including a Black Friday offer and more content, and is open to webinars, quiz funnels or in-person VSLs as long as action follows. James and JJ then compared funnel options: webinars start from scratch, while low-ticket to high-ticket matches the existing content and is clearer to build. They also locked the four recurring weekly calls and the email and event setup around them, and JJ walked Josh through the edit for the group-interview clip, which needs a stronger opening hook.

Bottom line

What matters

The team is moving from talking to execution. Each owner gets a clear lane: Josh owns content volume and affiliate output, James owns the funnel and the KPIs coming in from it, and Jalen starts recording content and helps on the setting side. The point of the KPIs is to see which funnel and which person is working, then taper back to what wins.

On direction, James argued that saying no to more channels will help. Webinars, in-person events, low-ticket, high-ticket and B2B agencies at once is too much, and the low-ticket to high-ticket path is the clearer one. A mid-ticket step between the two was floated. Webinars stay on the table, but the team will map everything out before committing.

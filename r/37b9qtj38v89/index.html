The short version

What we covered

Jia has been a setter and dialer since May and is tired of setting. She wants to close and learn from people who have done it. She is quoting permanent lights right now and said that is not why she got into sales. JJ asked what kind of opportunity she wants, and she said the niche matters less than strong fulfillment behind the product. JJ explained the twelve-week curriculum, the week three approval stage before closing placements, the job board with an in-house recruiter, internal teams and DealFuel. She decided to join during the call. JJ asked to see how she runs a call in tomorrow's first one-on-one, even without a role play behind it, and they set the schedule and sent access.

Bottom line

What matters

The deciding factor was clarity. JJ wants new closers to know which opportunity they are aiming at, because the curriculum and placement are built around it. Jia does not have a niche yet, but she knows what she does not want and what she values in an offer.

The week three approval stage is the gate for closing roles, since JJ vouches for people placed through the network. JJ said he sees her as further ahead than she thinks and wants to see her process on the first call.

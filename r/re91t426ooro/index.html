The short version

What we covered

Brandon Wilner is at the last stage of the hiring process for the Content Academy sales team. JJ told him the conversation with Mino is a relaxed one and that there is no roadblock from his side: Mino mainly needs to like him, so the best prep is to be honest. Next steps are a trial, usually around seven days, working mostly webinar leads. Brandon said he already did an objection-handling role play with Justin, using a binary identity line about the future version of yourself. He then talked through how he would run the offer: do discovery, find the goal, uncover the mind viruses around being nervous to post content, and close if they are in the right spot. JJ said he would reach out to Mino, then sent Brandon a Google Meet link on WhatsApp so he could jump on. They also agreed to a full 1:1 later in the week once Brandon has had a call or two.

Bottom line

What matters

The final step is a conversation with the founder, not a test. The trial that follows is about closing the leads you are given.

Brandon's plan for the offer is simple: connect it to the prospect's goal, find the beliefs that stop them from posting content, and close when they are in the right spot.

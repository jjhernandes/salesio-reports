The short version

What we covered

Dr. Simone Ellis is a retired dentist who sold a couple of her practices and now works as a dental business strategist for practice owners. Her clients have come mostly by word of mouth, and she described her sales approach as not methodical. She wants help closing individual deals and, more importantly, landing larger corporate accounts such as DSOs, and she does not know what to do when a call does not convert. JJ gave his background working with large creators and enterprise B2B companies, then laid out the call structure he teaches: respect first, likability later, current reality, problem awareness, solution awareness, future pacing, consequence, then a pitch tied to what came up in discovery. He explained the 12-week program done directly with him, with Evan Brown as B2B coach, SOPs she owns, and the option to hand sales off to a rep from the community. Simone travels around Thanksgiving, so a start in early December works. JJ will send the pricing and breakdown, and she will send a recorded call for him to review.

Bottom line

What matters

The gap is process, not skill. Simone already has expertise and referral trust, but no structured way to run a call or recover one that does not close. The proposed fix is a repeatable conversation structure and a system she can eventually hand to a rep.

The next step is concrete: she sends a recorded call, JJ reviews it the same day it arrives, and sends the pricing and 12-week structure. Timing is flexible, since she cannot start until early December.

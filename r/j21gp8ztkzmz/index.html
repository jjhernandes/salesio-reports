The short version

What we covered

JJ opened by inviting the group to a guest speaker session at 12 tomorrow, then ran a goal-setting round. Omar and Allan both aim for 30k a month, and Faris is targeting 20k a month before the end of the year with 100 to 150 cash collected this month. JJ pushed them to attach a specific number and clear proof to each goal, and showed his own method: a mock of the Stripe dashboard with the exact numbers, plus an audio to play at night and in the morning. He stressed that belief is what shifts results. Omar asked for the KPIs behind the goal (booked calls, show rate, close rate). Allan said that at his current close and show rates he needs about 20 calls a week but gets two to three a day. Allan will send JJ the seven-section frame his GrokBot filled in, and JJ will build a plan from the numbers. Faris still needs to set up his tracking sheet for the month's closes.

Bottom line

What matters

The goals are only useful if they turn into numbers someone can run. Omar put it this way: “I think I just need specific things to, like specific metrics to not achieve, but just like maybe KPIs that are required to hit this goal.” JJ agreed that booked calls, show rate and close rate are coming.

The call-volume gap is the open problem. Allan's math says he needs about 20 live calls a week, and he is getting two to three a day. JJ's push was on clarity and belief: get very specific, review it every day, and build the plan from real rates.

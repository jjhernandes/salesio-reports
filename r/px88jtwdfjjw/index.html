The short version

What we covered

The team opened with a few updates: a beta offer for a sales efficiency tool called Vana, and the group interview for a new closer. Justin said the extra closer is premature because it assumes ad spend doubles, so candidates stay in a backlog while he checks with Mino that night. The main block of the call came from Faris asking how to reframe prospects who want to try it alone before paying for coaching. Malachi suggested putting the prospect on the closer's calendar and leaving detail in the pre-call notes. Harrison shared a reframe he used the day before: the objection was really fear, so he asked whether the prospect wanted to face the struggles alone or with a coach. JJ then explained high ego, low competence prospects, who bring the confidence from their own business into a field they do not know. His answer was to win the expert frame first by asking questions that challenge the prospect's thinking without attacking their ego, and to ask early current-reality questions such as ICP, how long they have created content and whether they track lead attribution. The call ended with Malachi and Harrison trading how they spot and pre-handle these prospects.

Bottom line

What matters

The decision was to keep the closer hire on hold for now. Justin will check with Mino that night and report back.

The coaching point: do not fight a high ego prospect. Earn the expert frame first, then challenge. Questions about ICP, content history and lead attribution show the gap early, and the prospect starts asking the closer questions instead of pushing back.

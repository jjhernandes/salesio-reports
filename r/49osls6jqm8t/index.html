The short version

What we covered

Luis had gone through the intro content and a sample call and said it made sense. JJ checked that Luis understood what he was walking into before going further, then explained the program: 12 weeks of one-on-one work, the first four on learning to sell, then preparing for real opportunities, with access to the job board once he can run a sales call. Pricing is $8,250, split in two or three in-house payments with no interest, or third-party financing. Luis said the three-payment split was doable, but after thinking it through he said the timing is not right for him now. JJ asked whether it was fear or a real logistical limit, shared the view that there is rarely a perfect time, and left the door open without pressure. Luis said he wants to revisit in about a month or two.

{{Bottom line}}

What matters

Luis wants the program and said it opened his mind to a path he had not considered. The blocker is timing, not fit. He asked when he could start, was ready for the three-payment option, then decided the timing was off for now.

JJ kept it low pressure: if it does not work logistically, do not do it. The follow-up is to check back in a month or two, and Luis will reach out if he has questions sooner.

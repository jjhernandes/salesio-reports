The short version

What we covered

JJ said the goal cannot rest on him and Mino alone. The team needs its own clear reason to push, and financial incentives help but are not enough. He plans to give each closer a morning audio, a night audio and a snapshot of what they want, and to keep them accountable to it, using the idea that belief shapes identity and identity drives action. Mino asked for a reference point from someone who has collected at a similar price point, so the team can see what the daily inputs look like. JJ then listed three levers: dial back through the old pipeline in iClosed, build a VIP list of well-known entrepreneurs for a done-for-you offer, and package the editing offer into something more hands-on. JJ will build the plan, show it to Mino and Justin first, and share the link for a 12:30 session.

Bottom line

What matters

The tactical side is already clear and anyone can do the math. What is missing is urgency from within the team, so JJ will hold the targets at $1M a month and make the daily actions concrete.

Opportunity is not the concern. Momentum is: how intensely the team goes after pipeline, no-shows, reschedules and higher priced offers it has not touched yet.

What we covered

JJ asked about the lower cash collected, and the closers said there were a lot of no-shows and reschedules. Justin and the closers then walked through the upcoming 25k calls and how to pitch the new UGC agency offer. Justin's guidance was to use the offer doc, keep the doc open without screen sharing, and pitch the regular Academy to anyone who is not a clear fit. Omar asked how to see what prospects told the AI DM setter, and Justin said he would add the closers to the tool. The group ran wins and shout outs, then JJ and the Social Era team covered the new trial offer: a $1,500 break-even trial, $3,750 after results, and how to frame it. JJ said to present it as standard practice and use the prospect's own objection as the reason for the trial.

Bottom line

What matters

For Content Academy, the main work is pitching the right offer to the right prospect and following up on the no-shows. Justin's rule: use the offer doc, do not screen share, and default to the regular Academy when the prospect does not clearly fit the agency offer.

For Social Era, JJ's direction was to treat the trial as normal practice rather than something to apologize for. If a prospect hesitates, that hesitation is the reason for the trial.

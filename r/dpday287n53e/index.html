The short version

What we covered

JJ and James worked out the Black Friday plan for Sales.io. The idea is to send personalized offers to past students and run a public low-ticket offer with a Cyber Monday upsell. Because the program is mostly 1-on-1, the group is quiet, so a paid community tier could work alongside premium coaching. They settled on a done-for-you Grokbot package at $3,500 with community access for the year, with a $67 a month downsell and an early $47 price. James can get the package ready in 24 hours and run one group onboarding call. JJ wants own landing pages with automatic upsells instead of a school paywall. They also covered content: podcast days in the studio, twelve guest speakers, and a Q1 mastermind announced early. The aim is to have everything ready before Tuesday.

Bottom line

What matters

The main decision: package the Grokbot setup as a done-for-you offer with a clear price ladder, and sell it through custom landing pages and upsells.

The bigger point is planning. Black Friday came together late, so the next step is to map next year now: guest speakers, renewal and upsell systems, and the Q1 mastermind, so every event has a date and an owner.

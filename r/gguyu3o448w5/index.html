The short version

What we covered

Josh showed the platform and explained there are two content styles. The first is clips pulled automatically from JJ's Fathom calls: JJ reviews a list of recent calls, picks the ones to clip, and the platform edits them with captions and hooks in a couple of minutes. The second is raw talking head videos, where JJ talks straight to camera to one specific person. Videos are dragged onto a calendar and auto-posted as normal reels or trial reels. A Grok bot builds a running list of the common questions and belief shifts coming out of JJ's calls, so JJ knows what to film. The three archetypes are younger JJ, the charisma closer, and the rep struggling to break through. The monthly KPI is at least 50 clips from Fathom and at least 20 talking heads. Josh is aiming for a V1 tomorrow, with JJ, Jalen, Evan and the affiliates inside within the next week. Full-day OBS livestream recordings are too large for the platform as is, so Fathom stays the source for now.

Bottom line

What matters

The content engine now has two parts: create and document. Creating is the talking head videos, filmed to one specific person so the algorithm can find them. Documenting is the Grok list of questions and reframes plus the Fathom clips. Both run through the same platform, so nobody downloads, uploads or schedules by hand.

JJ needs to feed Grok the older calls, send over what happened to past clips, and block time to record. Josh needs to get the V1 live, resend the past clips in Slack and give JJ anchor points and on-screen hooks to film against.

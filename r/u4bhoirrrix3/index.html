The short version

What we covered

The sync opened with a closed sale from Ulani, then Mikaela went through two booked prospects: Huda, who has a strong application, and a business owner who already knew the $3,000 price and wants to be put in front of the right brands. The main block was a review of a call with a buyer who runs her own hat company and wanted UGC skills for it. JJ framed her as high ego with low confidence in how UGC works, and showed that obvious questions and a heavy frame make the closer look unsure. He also showed that answering each of her questions gives her control of the call, and gave a line to take it back. He added that sophisticated buyers do not feel pain the way typical leads do, and that they buy once they believe the seller is the right expert. The call ended with JJ promising to record the vision board audio himself and send it over.

Bottom line

What matters

The buyer is not after what most leads are after, which is replacing an income. Her bar to buy is higher, so the closer wins by sounding like the right expert and making what she came for look like what the program does, without lying about what it can do.<br><br>Control is the other half. JJ's point was that closers create their own resistance by asking obvious questions, then lose the call by answering everything the buyer asks. Acknowledge her, promise the answers, and ask your own questions.

What to fix

Do not ask obvious questions of a high-ego buyer

This buyer sounds sure of herself but is not confident about how UGC works. Obvious questions and a heavy frame make the closer sound unsure. Go expert frame and break the fourth wall.

Say it like thisGreat to meet you. I reviewed your notes. I'm sure you know how these calls go, you've probably been on a couple.
▶ 12:46

Do not answer every question she throws

Answering each of her questions hands her the conversation. Acknowledge her, say you will cover it later, then ask your own questions to get control back.

Say it like thisThese are all great questions. I'm going to answer all of this throughout the rest of the conversation. But I have a couple questions, because you have great awareness.
▶ 20:25

Get more context before taking her words at face value

When she says she wants to merge it with her company or avoid mixing brands, ask what she means. Without that the closer is guessing at the real goal.

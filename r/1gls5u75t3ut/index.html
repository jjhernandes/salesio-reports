The short version

What we covered

Luke walked through the launch event. He closed early, the middle got rough and he started doubting himself, and a few came through at the end. He named what made it hard: late calls running into the early morning, getting comfortable with the offer and objections, discoveries running longer than they should, and struggling to pull out a real reason and commitment to change, especially with buyers whose business was already going fine. He also noticed the pitch ran long for people who had already watched the webinar. Luke broke down his process clearly. JJ's read is that it was mostly Luke being in his head, not his process. Working alongside people you know brings a fear of their judgment, and that pushes you to mold yourself into a style that isn't yours. Luke's strength is connecting with people, so lean into that. JJ also recommended the book Not Nice, because people-pleasing is what a lot of closers struggle with.

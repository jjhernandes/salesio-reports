The short version

What we covered

The meeting covered process first. The pre-call script, where prospects send a video before the call, is landing well with the closers, and the live demos are going over well. Faris reported strong results on Mino AI setting calls: the last four he set all closed, and about eight in ten calls move on to a sales call. He asked for the same automated pre-call texts for the Mino AI setting calls, and Mino said it can run through SendBlue. Mino reminded the closers to ask every prospect for a sample video, because Mino AI Frontier works best when it can copy an editing style. The second half was a goals exercise. JJ asked each closer to tie their monthly goal to something with real emotional weight, then JJ will build the metrics, targets and morning and night audios around it.

Bottom line

What matters

JJ explained that performance peaks when the goal is hard but clear. The team gets the numbers, calls needed and weekly expectations from JJ, and the closers supply the personal reason behind the goal.

The closers were asked to get clear on their goals within the hour. JJ is free at 12:30 to help anyone who wants to work on it.

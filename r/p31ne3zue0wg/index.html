The short version

What we covered

Mino opened the first Tuesday sync of the month by setting the target: go for a million in revenue in November. The plan is to push Content Academy and the Mino AI Frontier offer together, deliver strongly on Content Academy, then upsell students into Frontier. He called the month so far a strong start, with the first two days alone well ahead of pace, and said he and Justin will focus on getting more booked calls to the closers and on putting more into ad spend as they scale. Content is running at roughly 1,100 posts for the month across the main pages, YouTube, LinkedIn and Twitter, plus a new Content Academy HQ page for clips. Jaire shared a win: a student whose trial expired was moved onto a custom payment plan so he could stay in. Yda and Jack were introduced as the new success managers, everyone on the team introduced their role, and the call ended with shoutouts and an announcement from JJ about a mindset coach session later in the week.

Bottom line

What matters

The month is built around two offers: Content Academy on the front end and Mino AI Frontier as the upsell. Closers and success managers each have a part. Closers fill the calendar with sold students, success managers deliver and surface upsell interest, and Justin and Mino work on getting more booked calls to the team.

JJ announced he is inviting a mindset coach (Mindtrepreneur) to speak with the sales team on Thursday or Friday, not confirmed yet. The session would be 60 to 90 minutes and CSMs are welcome to join. If the team hits a million dollars this month, a second guest speaker, Charlie Morgan, comes in.

The short version

What we covered

Mikaela and Ulani walked through the week after the Wednesday masterclass. Ulani saw about 10 to 12 bookings for Thursday, but several cancelled or were not qualified, so she is averaging one to two calls every other day and calls the webinar a net negative against her usual Thursday. Mikaela could not triage on Wednesday, but hit every booked lead the next morning and had decent conversations, though only four or five were booked. They also compared notes on prospects outside the US and Canada and where WhatsApp fits, since it is not connected in GHL yet. JJ pointed Mikaela at the cancelled-call list as the biggest return on her time, with a transparent report back if it turns out to be a waste. He said James is re-evaluating the webinar, Instagram should be back this week, and the team is working on a structure to send software users into the main offer. The call ended with a plan for a prospect asking to see the price before booking: send the brochure, keep the call mandatory, and frame it as the process that decides whether the program fits. Ulani and Mikaela still need to set up their GrokBots.

Bottom line

What matters

Volume dropped after the Wednesday masterclass, and Ulani reads the webinar as a net negative because Thursday normally books more. The fastest lever is not new traffic, it is the cancelled-call list. Mikaela should dial it, then report back honestly on whether it is worth the time.

Behind that, the structure is being rebuilt: James is re-evaluating the webinar setup, Instagram access should return this week, and JJ wants a better path from the software into the main offer for more lead flow. Prospects who try to skip the call get the brochure plus a clear message that the call is still required.

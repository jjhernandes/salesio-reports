What we covered

Your top objections are "it's too expensive," "we can't afford it," "we need time to think" and "how does this actually work." When JJ asked whether the hard part is finding the questions or the tension, you said the tension. So JJ started with the environment: sit back, stay relaxed, keep it warm, because a tense posture turns it into an awkward back and forth. Then he demoed on "it's too much money": isolate whether it's really the price or something else, use a drastic comparison and let the prospect take control, then lay out the payment options as clear choices. In your role-play, "it's expensive" turned out to be fear of repeating a bad coaching experience, which is exactly what isolating uncovers. You caught yourself repeating "can I ask you," and the hardest spot was the dad asking for a day. On the setting side, some kids hang up when you pitch the call with the closer or ask for mom and dad's numbers, so send JJ two of those recordings.

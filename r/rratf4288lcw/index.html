The short version

What we covered

JJ ran a check-in with the setters. Luisa dialed the no-show and canceled list and booked two people, but one lead who seemed very engaged has since stopped answering calls and WhatsApp. Sidney asked how likely a lead is to show after going silent, and JJ said show rate is above 60% overall and depends on lead attribution, meaning what the lead watched or did before booking. Josh and Fahima agreed that leads who booked and then missed are warmer than people who only signed up for a webinar, and Fahima noted some leads on the re-engage list have been called more than six times. JJ said to stop that. He plans to give the setters more opportunity by handing over leads from old closers that are stuck in the pipeline after a broken CRM automation. Sunday's webinar has about 15,000 registrants and JJ will confirm the details and when setters can start calling registrants.

Bottom line

What matters

Show-up rate comes down to lead awareness more than follow-up effort. JJ explained that a lead who watched one 30-second Instagram video is far less ready than one who watched a full hour-long YouTube video, and that affects both buy-in and showing up.

The setters' leads from Jaro's group chats often don't know who he is. They booked because they want help with UGC, so the connection to Jaro does not carry the show-up. The practical move is to work the warmer no-show and canceled list, book only serious people, and leave over-called leads alone.

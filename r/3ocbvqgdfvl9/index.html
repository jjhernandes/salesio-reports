The short version

What we covered

Naeem sells cold email for land developers and wanted to know whether his offer was good and whether he was leaving money on the table. He also said his calls start well but drift: halfway through, the prospect takes over the frame, asks what Naeem does, and Naeem starts over-explaining and speeding up. JJ asked about the actual flow of the call and found there is no fixed order of questions. Naeem described the pattern he sees, and prospects usually respond once he compares cold email to direct mail. JJ's read was that the offer is not the problem and the sale is. He used a walking-route analogy to explain why a process gets you to the same destination every time. JJ said he would send a sales process document and the first foundational program video, and Naeem agreed to watch it and keep implementing the plan he already has.

Bottom line

What matters

The main takeaway is that Naeem is closing, but by luck rather than by a system. In his words: “I think it's definitely the sales process. Like they are closing, but I feel like it's just luck. It's not like a system.” JJ agreed and said the offer is not the issue.

The priority is a clear call process before any objection handling or advanced tactics. JJ also pointed out that the beliefs Naeem brings to the call show up in how the call goes, and suggested the foundational video as the first step.

Louie: E-com Ad Account Blocked

  • Problem: Louie's e-com ad account was blocked after his first sale, halting momentum. He is resistant to paying $300–$500/mo for an agency account, citing financial risk. ▶ 3:50
  • JJ's Coaching: ▶ 6:31
  • Reframe Risk as Data Acquisition: View the cost as an investment to gather data and learn, not as a potential loss.
  • Calculate Break-Even: Determine the number of sales needed to cover the investment.
  • Embrace Anti-Fragility: Expect and plan for setbacks as an inevitable part of the entrepreneurial journey.

Iwin: Finding Purpose

  • JJ's Coaching: ▶ 6:31

  • Set Service-Oriented Goals: Pursuing goals for the benefit of others provides deeper, more sustainable fulfillment than purely selfish pursuits.

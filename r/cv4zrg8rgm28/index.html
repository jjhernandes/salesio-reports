The short version

What we covered

We dug into one question: how deep should a setter go in discovery? JJ's line is simple. Once you're challenging their beliefs or subjective language ("Why 10k?", "What makes you believe you can do that?"), you're doing the closer's job. Match depth to how problem-aware they are, qualify with the 3-criteria frame, and book. You also locked in your plan to write the Ideal Intro and to go deep on Behavior Ops Manual and Reality Transurfing.

What to fix

Stop at the closer's line

If a question challenges their belief or subjective language, it belongs on the closing call. Park it.

Say it like this"What's the goal?" → "10k." → "Why 10k? What makes you believe you can do that?" → "Love it. Here's how we work…"
▶ 5:52

Don't peak them on the setting call

If they're asking "how do I move forward?" on your call, their emotion peaks too early and they're flat on the close. You saw this 3 weeks ago.

Say it like this"Sounds like this is exactly what the demo is for. Let's get you in front of the team so you get the full picture."
▶ 8:08

Match depth to awareness

High problem-awareness → 2 questions, the 3-criteria qualification frame, book (~15 min). Still skeptical 10 minutes in → that's when deeper is right.

Say it like this"Love it. Here's how we operate. There are 3 things we look for: you can close, you have capacity, and…"

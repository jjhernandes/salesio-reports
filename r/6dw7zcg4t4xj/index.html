What we covered

The Problem: Outcome-Attachment & Limiting Beliefs

  • Outcome-Attachment: Focusing on outcomes over inputs signals scarcity. ▶ 12:28
  • Analogy (Bodybuilding): Progress comes from the work (cardio, diet), not from fantasizing about the result.
  • Limiting Beliefs: Self-imposed limits on capability. ▶ 5:22
  • JJ's Example: JJ initially capped his business at 4 teams and 40 students, but shifting his mindset and focusing on a single, scalable system (training → placement) allowed him to exceed those limits.

The Solution: Inputs, Visualization & Speed

  • High-Volume Inputs: Provide the data and feedback needed to find what works. ▶ 23:37
  • Example (Comedian): Posting 40 videos/day revealed a winning format, leading to 800k views and 80k followers/day.
  • Speed: A critical input that creates opportunity. ▶ 20:54
  • Example (Influencer Deal): Moving quickly to book a call with a 50M-follower influencer secured a deal worth an estimated $200k/month.
  • Visualization ("Woo"): Use Neville Goddard's "felt concept" to feel the outcome. ▶ 28:00
  • Practice: Spend 5–10 minutes daily vividly imagining and feeling the desired result.

Application: Handling Objections & Tension

  • Challenge Financial Objections: Assume "I can't transfer funds" is a stall tactic. ▶ 58:37
  • Tactic: State company policy requires an immediate deposit to secure the spot.
  • Rationale: This frames the action as non-negotiable and tests the prospect's commitment.
  • Confront Tension: Discomfort is a sign of growth. ▶ 71:54
  • Origin: Often stems from childhood patterns of avoiding conflict.
  • Practice: Lean into uncomfortable conversations in real life to build composure.
  • Adem's Insight: Ask, "Is this how my ideal self would operate?" to reframe the situation and act from a place of authority.

Win: Courtney's $10k Month

  • Courtney shared a major win: hitting a $10k month after setting it as a "delusional goal" one month prior. ▶ 95:08
  • New Goal: $20k/month. ▶ 95:47

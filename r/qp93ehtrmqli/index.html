What we covered

JJ met with Josh, Jalen and James. Evan could not make it. Starting Monday the team will run a rapid-fire review, with Josh owning content volume and affiliate output, and James owning funnel KPIs and where leads come from. Josh said the software is nearly done, so KPIs can go out to everyone, affiliates included. JJ wants a very clear KPI for each person and wants to see how it correlates with revenue. They agreed to push for the last 15 days of the month and think about a Black Friday offer. JJ reviewed a clip with Josh and agreed to add a hook up front. With James, the team locked in four recurring weekly events and talked through whether to focus on low ticket to high ticket, webinars, or in-person masterminds.

Bottom line

What matters

The goal is to get everyone moving in the same direction with clear numbers. Josh owns content output, James owns funnel tracking, and the Monday review keeps each owner accountable. JJ said the team has talked enough and wants action behind the ideas for the rest of the month.

James favored low ticket to high ticket because it matches JJ's current content and is easier to test than webinars. JJ agreed that saying no to some channels will help, and said they would map it out together.

The short version

What we covered

JJ shared news from an emergency meeting with the founders: the $3,750 offer drops to $1,500 as a test, with the same guarantee and the same delivery. The higher-priced package is off the table for now, and the closers should run the lower price on every call this week so the founders get real data. JJ explained that a lower price should lift close rate enough to make up for the lower commission per sale. Iwin asked whether lead volume doubles with the cut. JJ said the founders think show rate is the first hole to fix, then more leads. Iwin and Chris pushed back that show rate is not their problem, and Chris backed it up with his tracking sheet. They agreed to send tracking sheets so the founders can see where leads actually drop off. A setter is coming in, and before that Iwin and Chris will re-offer older leads at the new price. The call ended with a coaching question on a lead who kept declining the meeting invite, and Iwin asked JJ to review a call recording today.

Bottom line

What matters

The lower price is a test to gather data, not a final decision. JJ asked the closers to run it on every call this week and stay honest about what they see. Iwin said the new price needs at least three or four booked calls a day to make sense.

The open question is show rate versus lead quality. Chris and Iwin both say show rate is fine, and Chris's sheet shows the drop happens earlier, at contact and booking. Tracking sheets will settle it. In-person sessions with the founders are still being explored, and Iwin will join online.

In my words

The real conversation

The stall is fear, not research. As I told you: “Fear is always going to stop them, but it's always a belief, right?” She does not need more options, she is trying to avoid making a mistake, and you can name that without attacking her.

The way in is to make it about who she wants to become: “Mariah number 2 would most likely take the step, regardless of fear.” Keep it soft and keep it in her story. “We think in narrative. We think in stories.” And on the tech side, let the bots carry the load: “You should have it handle pretty much everything for you.”

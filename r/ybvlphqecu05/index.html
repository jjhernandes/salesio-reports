What we covered

Problem: Rigid Process & Burnout

  • Mariah's process is rigid, not adaptable to diverse prospect needs. ▶ 6:43
  • Analogy: Forcing prospects into a pre-defined box instead of exploring boxes together.
  • Rationale: This causes conversations to feel chaotic and leads to Mariah's self-doubt about her process.
  • This rigidity creates burnout and call resistance. ▶ 23:39
  • Root Cause: A subconscious anxiety from past negative experiences (e.g., the "Sideswan" call).
  • Impact: This anxiety drains energy and creates a feeling of being stuck.
  • JJ noted Mariah's demeanor is consistently "stern" or "boss babe," regardless of the prospect's energy, which creates a mismatch. ▶ 22:01

Solution: Energy Matching & Adaptability

  • The new strategy is to build rapport first, then introduce authority. ▶ 19:54
  • Method: Match the prospect's energy (e.g., smile with a smiling prospect, be direct with a serious one).
  • Rationale: This builds trust and makes Mariah more receptive to the prospect's needs.
  • Authority is established gradually, not from the start. ▶ 19:54
  • Technique: Use a verbal bridge to transition from peer to guide (e.g., "I know we've been friendly, but I'll also be a leader for you...").
  • Rationale: This creates a more natural flow and avoids the prospect feeling forced.
  • This approach is uniquely effective for Mariah's female-to-female sales context, where connection is a primary driver of trust and purchase decisions. ▶ 17:30

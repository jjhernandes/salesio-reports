The short version

What we covered

JJ joined a call that Malachi and Bryan Jabalera were already on, and the three of them started with mutual connections in the closing and setting world, including Axel, who came through Sales.io. Bryan explained that he is the face of his own brand and does not enjoy recording content, which is why he related to the way Mino uses AI to cut down on the content he has to record. He said he runs a Spanish-language offer with very low ad costs, and his only real issue is sales operations, specifically closer talent. JJ explained what Sales.io does: it trains and places closers, and he runs sales for Mino and one other team. JJ pointed out that many Sales.io closers speak Spanish and offered to introduce talent at no cost. They agreed Bryan would text JJ so he can pass along intros, and that JJ would be in Miami in about two weeks.

Bottom line

What matters

This was a relationship call, not a sales call. Nobody was pitched, and JJ was clear he was not trying to sell Bryan anything. The useful outcome is the talent conversation: Bryan has the ads and demand figured out, and says “my only issue, low-key, my only issue is sales operation.”

JJ’s offer is simple and free: Bryan texts him, and JJ introduces closers from the Sales.io network, including Spanish speakers. A follow-up message is the only next step.

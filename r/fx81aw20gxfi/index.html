Objection Handling: "Partner" & "Think About It"

  • Problem: Prospects use the "partner" or "think about it" objection to defer the decision. ▶ 41:29
  • Ineffective Method → Pre-handling: Asking "Does anyone else need to be involved?" can introduce an objection the prospect hadn't considered. ▶ 42:40
  • Effective Method → Reframe the Decision: ▶ 41:29
  • Step 1: Acknowledge. "I understand you want to talk to your partner."
  • Step 2: Clarify Involvement. "Do they have any direct involvement in your company?"
  • Step 3: Offer a Choice. "Is this more about deferring responsibility, or do you need to review the numbers with them?" If it's the numbers, you can walk through them right there on the call.
  • Step 4: Use an Analogy. Frame the decision as facing a task head-on or delaying it, and highlight the benefits of acting now.

The short version

What we covered

Mino and Justin opened with the capacity picture: every closer was booked on more than 50 calls last week, one on 77, so closers are the bottleneck and the team set a goal of eight by the end of the month. JJ was fully on board and said it is better to plan ahead than react. Justin debriefed the latest round of closer interviews and the group agreed to run another group interview on Thursday at 4pm, with a hiring story from Mino and more prep time for applicants. Justin will send invites to about 60 people, and applications must include a Loom. Justin then walked through a lead routing plan for trial closers: international leads and leads with no budget go to them, and they need to average $300 cash per call over 15 calls to pass. The group also covered setters dialing leads, a weekly setter meeting, a toggle that hands qualified leads from the AI setter to a person, and a low-ticket downsell idea for leads who cannot get financing.

Bottom line

What matters

JJ said the plan to reach eight closers is realistic and that sales leaders are more reactive than proactive, so building routing, backup calendars and a trial lane now keeps the team ahead of demand. His job is to work with the team's game plan unless he sees a real risk, and he does not see one here.

For the hiring push, JJ asked for more lead time and more visible momentum: tell the story of a record week and a team at capacity, show real results, and keep it exclusive. Candidates share these posts in closer communities, so the post does the recruiting. Justin and JJ agreed to keep the Thursday interview under two hours and split it in two if more than 35 people show.

The real conversation

The first thing is recognition. I'd open with something simple: “Hey, obviously we didn't end off on the best foot yesterday. We both recognize that, right?” Then “you hear him out, give him his power.” After that you tell him you want a good working relationship, and you ask what he wants from here.

I was also direct about the process: “Definitely should have let me know before you had that conversation.” We could have worked through it together. On drawing the line, my advice was to “stand your ground and be like, no.” Don't be afraid of the repercussions. You don't owe anyone a return to the old way of working.

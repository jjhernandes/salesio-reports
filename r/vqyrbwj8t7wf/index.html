The short version

What we covered

JJ updated the team on closer hiring: the goal is to reach eight closers by the end of the month, assuming bookings keep rising, because closer capacity is now the constraint. Interviews so far were arranged with almost no notice, so candidates will get more lead time, and another interview is set for Thursday. Allan raised a dip in bookings after a recent change and said ad spend was fixed afterward, and JJ noted Malachi took 70 calls last week. Omar asked how to get his AI assistant fully optimized for follow-up beyond the emails it already sends, and JJ said he can simply tell it what to do, including texts and replies on social. Allan asked when to close a prospect he doubts will succeed versus when to DQ. JJ said the job is to support, but also to use discretion to DQ, and to frame it around priorities and revisit later. JJ closed by recommending the book Not Nice.

Bottom line

What matters

Closer capacity is the bottleneck, so the plan is to scale the team as bookings grow. Candidates will get more notice and the next interview is Thursday.

On qualification, there is no hard rule. Closers are expected to use their own judgment, and when a prospect is not a fit, the message should be about priorities and timing, not about their limitations.

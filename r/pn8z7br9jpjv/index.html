What we covered

JJ opened the call by realizing the webinar test was happening today instead of next Wednesday. Patrick confirmed he had already switched the reminder and replay workflows last week, so everything was set. The closers asked how the day would run and were told it is the same as usual, just earlier in the week, with the calendar filling up as people register. Mikaela flagged that an automation promises a workbook that was removed a few weeks ago, and Patrick will remove that message and send the link to the one person who asked. She also got a plan for what to text pre-webinar leads who do not reply. Patrick is finishing a commas notification that should be live before the webinar. JJ explained that Wednesdays and Sundays usually perform best, so the team will test Wednesday and use Sunday as the backup, and he and Patrick agreed the Sunday encore did not do well. Kirsty is traveling and will miss this Wednesday but is back for Saturday the 14th. The rest of the call was the team getting access to the Sales.io program and a mindset coach call that JJ is scheduling.

Bottom line

What matters

The webinar moves to Wednesday as a test. It avoids pushing into the weekend, where no-shows and cancellations have been bad, and it means the team relies less on weekend setters and closers.

If Wednesday works, it stays. If not, Sunday is the fallback, and the Sunday encore webinar is not coming back.

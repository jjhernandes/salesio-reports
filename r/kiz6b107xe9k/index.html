The short version

What we covered

Amber was introduced to JJ through a mutual contact. A partner company wants about 100 sales reps and the bottleneck is not enough consultants, so Amber has been reaching out to her network, which holds roughly 300 closers. Her gap is the recruiting pipeline: a bot pulls interested people into the first stage and then nothing happens, and she has never run a group interview. JJ broke down how his teams hired reps in bulk: a basic application with hidden instructions to test who follows directions, a group interview of around 30 people, a 10-minute stretch with the hosts off camera to watch who leads and who goes quiet, cuts with a short reason, a 1 to 5 rating in Slack, and then further interviews and a seven-day trial with a KPI. Amber summarized the flow back and said it was enough to implement now. JJ will send the SOP and the recording, and offered to help run the first interviews.

Bottom line

What matters

The recruiting problem is a process problem, not a people problem. Amber has the talent in her DMs, and what is missing is a system that filters it. The group interview does that filtering in one session.

The reason it works is pre-framing. Candidates earn their spot through several stages, so they value the role more. JJ framed it as knowing the strengths you need and the weaknesses you are willing to tolerate.

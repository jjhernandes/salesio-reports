The short version

What we covered

Iwin opened by describing the system he took from a recent call review: every weekend he writes down the three things he keeps getting feedback on, drills only those, runs role plays in any gaps, then reviews and picks three new ones. He also shifted his intention before each call, focusing on helping the prospect get across the fence instead of the commission, and he reported five closes in five days including two double-close days. One prospect left the call right after the payment link was sent and blocked him, but he said it did not shake him because of the new intention. He also ran a mock with a new teammate and said the offer is a no-brainer. Chris walked through two short, awkward calls and three leads that said yes but have not paid. The advice was to stay certain at the end of the pitch even when the prospect cuts it short and move straight to payment and onboarding. JJ closed by recommending the book Not Nice and the call ended with the next 1:1 moved to 12:30.

Bottom line

What matters

The offer is working. Pricing and structure are now balanced, and the reps are closing because their intention is clear, not because they are pushing harder.

The gap is certainty at the close. When a call gets cut short or goes sideways, the move is to treat it as normal and lead with the next step: payment first, then onboarding.

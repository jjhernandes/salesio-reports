The short version

What we covered

JJ opened by asking what the sales team needs to make the million-dollar month a certainty. Allan and Omar said the sales side is solid and the main gap is taking more calls. Malachi said he mostly needs a stronger follow-up process for leads who do not close, and Omar asked for the same. JJ pushed the team to escalate stuck leads quickly and to bring their own ideas. Mino announced a webinar slide with bonuses for people who join afterward, including a free 1:1 consultation and 10 hooks. Allan raised how to re-pitch older leads who were quoted lower prices, and Mino agreed to grandfather them in with an end-of-month deadline. The team also discussed showing the coaches' credibility to prospects without building a full deck. JJ will build the follow-up process and a daily exercise for the team.

Bottom line

What matters

The team does not need more support on the sales side; they need more volume and a better system after the call. Follow-up should be personalized to the lead's situation and built around the trigger that made them book in the first place.

Older leads will be re-offered at the price and term they were pitched, as long as they act by the end of the month. For credibility, the plan is a short one-pager of who the coaches are, not a deck that carries the sale.

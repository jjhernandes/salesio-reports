In my words

The real conversation

Your biggest gap is the follow-up questions. When he said his agency "supposedly" ranks him higher, that was the moment to dig. As I said: "I would have just been like, supposedly? Like, what do you mean? Like, what are you unsure about?" Then, when he says "that's why I'm here," it is not an answer. "It's like, no, but why actually? Is it that you don't know where to go? Is it that you don't have an alternative?"

You are not trying to make him feel stupid. "You educate a little bit, and you give them some value." Show him a perspective he has not considered, that is The Challenger Sale idea, and he gives you the authority.

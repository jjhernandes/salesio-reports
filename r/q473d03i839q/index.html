The short version

What we covered

Iwin opened with a question outside the usual sales process: how to stop talking to everyone like a bro and switch cleanly into a professional tone. JJ said it comes down to training yourself to speak that way all the time, not only on calls, and not being afraid of sounding formal. Chris joined and added that identifying your audience and using their language makes the conversation move faster. Chris then asked Iwin for recordings of calls where a partner objection came up and the buyer still paid. Iwin described four such calls and agreed to send three. Iwin also asked how to use the pattern interrupt Evan teaches when humor is not his style. JJ said to take the principle and build something that sounds like him, practice it in normal conversations first, and show it on tomorrow's call. Chris wants to roleplay the new pitch and package, and they set that for later today.

Bottom line

What matters

The fix for tone is repetition, not a script. Speak the same way on and off calls so the switch is not a performance, and match the language of the person in front of you.

The pattern interrupt is a principle, not a joke. Anything that stops the prospect from rushing past discovery works, as long as it sounds like you and has something real under it when they push back.

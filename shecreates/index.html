Section 05Honest read

A Straight Read on October

The good, and the part we're fixing.

05

The front of the funnel is growing: more applications, more qualified applications and more booked calls per day than before we started. Cash hasn't caught up yet. Whop shows $21,800 collected Oct 1–25 from 10 new students, after $33,700 and 22 new students in September. Webinar registrations fell by about half (947 vs 1,975), and live attendance went from 84–157 a webinar in September to 48–93 in October. Failed financing approvals also cost us paid-in-full sales, which is why a backup processor is being added. Now that every call result is recorded (since Oct 15), we can finally see the real picture: a 37% show rate and a 40% close rate. That's the starting line for levers 1 and 2, not a ceiling.

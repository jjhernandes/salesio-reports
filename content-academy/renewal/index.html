Section 05Honest read

A Straight Read on October

The good, and the part we're fixing.

05

The sales team collected $362,736 in October, its best month so far. Revenue was $425,354, up 3.6% on September. Closer calls on the calendar hit 437, the most of any month, and pricing changed mid-month. Conversion fell: close rate dropped to 33%, from 47% in August and 45% in September, and below July’s 41%. Show rate was 68%. So October’s cash came from more calls, not better conversion, and cash per booked call fell from $1,002 in September to $830. Levers 1 and 2 are built to fix exactly this.

The math, backward from $1M

  1. $1,000,000 or a little more = about $918k from closer calls + about $108k from everything else (≈ $1.03M, the same model as the Path to $1M calculator above).
  2. $918k ÷ $3,400 average sale = ~270 sales a month.
  3. 270 sales ÷ 45% close = ~600 calls held.
  4. 600 held ÷ 80% show = ~750 closer calls on the calendar (~34 every business day).
  5. 750 calls ÷ ~100 held per closer = 6 closers at full load. Today there are 4.
  6. From Oct 1 to 25, 62% of all bookings reached a closer (355 of 577). At that rate, 750 closer calls means ~1,220 bookings a month.est
  7. From Oct 1 to 25, ads cost about $142 per booking ($81,858 ÷ 577). At that cost, ~1,220 bookings is ~$173k a month in ad spend, if the cost per booking holds.est
  8. Every CSM who hits the $20k a week upsell goal adds ~$87k a month, which lowers how many closer calls the $1M month needs.est
1

Get show rate back to 80%

Show rate is the biggest bottleneck right now. At October's volume, 68% to 80% alone is about +18% cash from the same bookings.

  • Rebuild the pre-call sequences (texts, emails, VSL before the call)
  • Setter confirmation calls on every booking, same-day sets from the dialer
  • Booking form questions that qualify on editing pain before the call
Target: 80% show. Check weekly.
2

Make every call worth $1,224

Close 33% to 45% and the average sale $3,241 to $3,400.

  • One clear offer ladder: $5k 3-month combo to enter, $8k Content Academy Frontier as the main offer, in-house payment plans as standard
  • Frontier demo in under 5 minutes on every webinar call
  • Fast close for decision-ready buyers, weekly call reviews on lost calls
Target: 45% close, $3,400 average sale, $1,224 per call.
3

Build the capacity for 750 calls

The demand is there. The team and the calendar need to be ready for it.

  • Grow from 4 to 6 closers through the hiring funnel, with a setter-to-closer path
  • Scale ads toward ~1,220 bookings while holding ROAS (September was 3.20, Oct 1 to 25 was 1.82)
  • CSM upsell engine at $20k a week per CSM
Target: 6 closers, ~750 closer calls a month.

Section 05Honest read

A Straight Read on October

The part that went the wrong way, what the data can and can't tell us, and what we're doing about it.

05

September proved the desk can close. October hasn't, yet. On the sheets, October has 21 closing calls, 9 held (42.9%) and 1 win ($389 deposit). The calendar didn't dry up: GoHighLevel shows 99 appointments Oct 1–25, close to September's pace (3.96 a day vs 4.23). Three things are going on. Show rate fell: on the closer's sheet that's been logged every month, show rate went from 83.3% in August to 73.7% in September to 42.9% in October, and our Oct 19 review traced it to the funnel change: the VSL funnel ran 80–88% show, the website funnel 35–40%. Yeses aren't paying: two buyers said yes in October and never paid, which is why every close now takes a $389 deposit. October is under-tracked: one closer's sheet stops at Sep 24 (31 days with nothing logged), and GoHighLevel never gets a "showed" or "won" status, so October's real number is likely higher than the sheets show. We can't tell how much until the sheets are filled in.
